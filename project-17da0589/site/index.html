about the atlas

This atlas presents the examination Kinetic friction and the human Avatar (FRICTION_EXAMINATION.md in the project). Every knowledge element can be shown in five forms (words, graph, symbols, animation, interactive) at five depths (everyday, image, mechanism, formal, limits). At knowledge level n you see the first n forms × the first n depths, so n² representations. Raising n only adds the 2n + 1 new cells; nothing you have seen disappears. These counting facts are proved in Lean (RequestProject/Site/Grid.lean).

Elements marked proved rest on a Lean theorem in RequestProject/Friction. The Babel element is interpretive: its curves are illustrative, not measured. No theorem here establishes a claim about consciousness, myth or language.

The spoken tour uses your browser's speech synthesis. If it has none, the captions advance on a reading timer.

Links: #at=element/depth/form&n=3 opens a cell, #tour=5 starts the tour at step 5, and #share=… carries a community submission.
